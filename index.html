00 Verdict

3600 Elo is unreachable. Not by a tuning margin — by ~151× in parameter capacity, and by a second, independent gap that capacity cannot close.

Wall 1 — capacity

Fit a log-linear curve through the three public policy-only (1-node, no search) anchors:

NetworkParamsPolicy-only Elo
Maia-1900 (6×64 ResNet)1.2M~1900
Leela 20×25622M~2575
Leela BT4 (transformer)~200M~2850
Elo ≈ −694 + 433 · log₁₀(Nparams)

Solving for 3600 gives 8.2 × 10⁹ parameters. Extrapolating this far past the anchors is unsound — the curve certainly flattens — so treat that figure as a generous lower bound on the requirement. The connectome offers 2.7M weights at one-per-edge, or 54.5M at one-per-synapse (§2.1). That is 151× short at best, 2,400× short at worst.

Wall 2 — search

Stockfish's 3600 is not a network property. It is a ~10⁸ nodes/sec alpha-beta search to depth 30+, with a 23–69M-parameter NNUE as the leaf evaluator. No policy network of any size currently reaches 3600 at one node; the best measured 1-node play is ~2850–2900. A policy network, however large, is ~700 Elo short before capacity enters. One fly-brain forward pass is, by construction, one node.

At the realistic ceiling of §2.4 (~1,850 Elo), the expected score against Stockfish-max is 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ — about one non-loss per 42,000 games.

What this project is instead

REFRAMED TARGETProduce the first measured capacity↔competence curve for a biological connectome held topologically fixed, using chess as the complexity dial — and ship a fly that plays real, legal, non-trivial chess at 1,500–1,900 Elo. The deliverable is the curve and the ablation set (§7). The Elo is the demo.

The blackjack-dealer checkpoint

Verdict: keep the config, discard the weights. Net liability as an initialization.

A blackjack dealer is a lookup table: ~20 reachable states (hard/soft totals 4–21) × 2 actions, gated by one rule — hit below 17. Total task entropy ≈ 7 bits. Training 55M parameters to convergence on a 7-bit target does not leave the network warmed up; it drives it into a low-dimensional attractor with severe rank collapse and a large dormant-unit fraction. This is the loss-of-plasticity failure documented by Dohare et al. (2024, Nature): networks trained to convergence on a trivial task measurably lose the ability to learn a hard one. Fine-tuning from there, for a task needing ~10⁶× more entropy, is worse than a fresh init.

Falsifiable test, week 1. Compute per-region effective rank (‖W‖F²/‖W‖₂²) and dormant fraction (<0.1 Hz over 10k random inputs). If effective rank < 5% of dimension or dormant fraction > 30% in the central brain, the weights are confirmed dead.

What is reusable, and worth ~30–50 GPU-hours: the neuron parameters already tuned to a stable non-saturating regime (that is Stage 0, already done); the I/O harness — sensory driver, spike-count decoder, CSR loader and its index layout; and the negative results on which surrogate-gradient scales explode. If you insist on warm-starting weights, use shrink-and-perturb (Ash & Adams 2020) at λ=0.3, σ=0.01·std(W), budget one 20-GPU-hour A/B, and expect it to match a fresh init rather than beat it.

Baseline calibration from Shiu et al. 2024 / philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model: τm = 20 ms, Vrest = −52 mV, Vθ = −45 mV, refractory 2.2 ms, τsyn = 5 ms, unitary weight 0.275 mV. Published values are the start of the Stage-0 search, not fixed points.

Dale's law. W_ij = s_i · softplus(a_ij), with s_i = ±1 fixed by the FlyWire neurotransmitter prediction for the presynaptic neuron (Eckstein et al. 2024: ~48% ACh, ~22% GABA, ~22% Glu, ~4% monoamines). Only a_ij trains, so a neuron can never flip sign.

Adaptation. 20% of central-brain neurons get an adaptive threshold, τadapt log-uniform over 20 ms – 2 s (Bellec et al. 2018). Mean shortest path in FlyWire is ~2.8–3.5 hops, so computational depth must come from time, and pure LIF over 96 steps has a ~20 ms memory horizon. Declared relaxation: this adds one scalar dynamic variable per neuron. It adds no neurons and no connections.

Fidelity ↔ trainability: three tiers, B chosen

Tier B keeps every property the claim depends on — real adjacency, real neurotransmitter signs, discrete spikes, biological time constants — and drops only what is unobservable at the readout. Tier C is used, but it is honestly "a sparse RNN with a fly-shaped mask." It is a pretraining device and a control, never the headline. Both numbers get reported.

Why not Brian2 or NEST: neither differentiates. BPTT needs autodiff through the full 96-step unroll; Brian2 standalone and NEST give forward simulation only, and GeNN/Brian2CUDA give fast forward without gradients. Brian2 is retained as a cross-validation oracle — forward-simulate 1,000 trials in both, require max per-neuron spike-count divergence < 2%.

2.5 What is not the bottleneck

  • Output channel. ~1,300 descending neurons × ~2 bits/trial = 2.6 kbit; selecting among ~35 legal moves needs 5.1 bits. 500× overhead.
  • Input channel. 768 retinotopic units × ~3 bits = 2.3 kbit against ~160 bits of board state.
  • Compute. ~1,050 GPU-hours total. The 24 GB GPU is not the constraint.
  • Depth. 96 timesteps of recurrence gives effective depth ~96, well past the ~20 layers strong chess nets use.

The bottleneck is weight entropy and fixed topology, and nothing else. That is a publishable finding on its own.

03 I/O encoding

3.1 Board → spikes

Piece placement → retinotopic visual pathway. Each optic lobe has ~800 medulla columns. Allocate 12 columns per square per eye: 64 × 12 = 768 columns, ~96% of one lobe. Column k of square s is driven iff piece type k occupies s (6 white + 6 black planes). This is exactly the standard 768-feature chess bitboard mapped onto the fly's actual retinotopy — the board is literally projected onto the eye.

  • Rate code, Poisson, 80 Hz active / 2 Hz background, sustained across all 96 steps. The board is static within one decision, so rate is correct.
  • Plus a synchronous onset burst at t = 0–2 ms across active columns, giving the network a phase reference. Assumption that this materially speeds convergence — ablate in Stage 1 and report.
  • R1–R8 → lamina L1–L5 → medulla. Both lobes get the same board; bilateral redundancy is a feature (see the mirror test, §6.4).

Everything else → non-visual pathways. The antennal lobe has 51 glomeruli; 17 are allocated.

Splitting modalities forces convergence to happen in the central brain (LH, MB, SIP/SMP) rather than letting the network treat 785 undifferentiated input lines as one vector. Assumption — ablate against a flat all-inputs-to-medulla control.

Timing. 96 steps × 0.5 ms = 48 ms, biologically motivated: fly looming-evoked escape runs on a 30–60 ms sensorimotor latency, so 48 ms is one fly decision. The readout window is the last 32 steps, after the onset transient has crossed the ~3-hop mean path length several times over.

3.2 Spikes → move

Factored readout over descending neurons. ~1,300 DNs exist; a 1-hot over 1,968 UCI move encodings is impossible. Instead: 64 DN groups → from-square logits, 64 → to-square, 5 → promotion piece. 133 groups × 10 DNs = 1,330 DNs ≈ the full population.

logit(g) = spikes(g, last 32 steps) / 10  ·  score(m) = logit_from[f] + logit_to[t] + logit_promo[p]

A second, separately-pooled ~40 DNs carry a scalar WDL value head over the same window.

3.3 Illegal moves — and the honest accounting

  1. Training: mask illegal moves before the softmax, standard AlphaZero practice. The mask comes from python-chess, externally.
  2. Play: argmax over legal moves.
  3. Reporting: always publish the unmasked legal-move rate — the fraction of held-out positions where the argmax over all 4,672 encodings is legal with no mask. Target ≥ 95% by M4.
DECLARED RELAXATIONThe legality oracle is external and not learned. Learning legality from scratch would consume an estimated 30–40% of available weight entropy to encode rules known perfectly a priori, for an estimated −500 Elo; every strong engine does the same. The unmasked-rate metric exists so this relaxation cannot quietly inflate the claim that the fly knows chess.

04 Learning rule

Chosen: surrogate-gradient BPTT, full unroll over T = 96, gradient checkpointing every 12 steps. Surrogate: triangular (Bellec et al. 2018), ∂S/∂v ≈ γ·max(0, 1 − |v − V_θ|/V_θ), γ = 0.3, with fast-sigmoid (SuperSpike) as fallback. Zenke & Vogels (2021) show results are robust to surrogate shape but sensitive to scale, so γ is swept in Stage 0 then frozen.

RejectedWhy
e-propO(1)-in-time memory and biologically plausible, but eligibility traces discard the non-local-in-time gradient, costing 10–30% on tasks with real temporal structure. T = 96 is short enough that full BPTT fits in 24 GB. Held in reserve if the memory budget breaks.
Reward-modulated STDPThree-factor rules with a scalar global reward have gradient variance scaling with parameter count. At d = 55.2M the sample complexity is ~10⁴–10⁶× BPTT's. Dead on arrival.
Evolution strategiesVariance also scales with d: ~10⁴–10⁵ perturbations × ~10⁴ updates ≈ 10⁹ simulations ≈ 10⁵ GPU-hours. Dead for the weights — but retained for the ~470 neuromodulatory gains and a rank-16 per-neuropil modulation (~10⁴ params), tuned by CMA-ES as an outer loop. That is where ES is the right tool.

Credit assignment

The key move is never requiring long-horizon credit assignment at all in Stages 0–3. One training example = one position → one move; the episode is 48 ms. Game-level credit arrives through the target (Stockfish's evaluation), not through a return propagated over 80 moves. Only Stage 4 does RL, with 1-step TD against a bootstrapped value head from a 32-simulation search — still no long horizon.

Within the 48 ms trial the risks are ordinary RNN pathologies: vanishing, mitigated by ALIF adaptation spanning 20 ms – 2 s and monitored as ‖∂L/∂h₀‖ / ‖∂L/∂h_T‖ (must stay in [10⁻³, 10³]); exploding, handled by global grad-norm clip at 1.0 and a Stage-0 constraint holding the linearized Jacobian's spectral radius at 0.95–1.05; and dead gradient from silence, handled by a firing-rate regularizer λ_r·Σ(r_i − 5 Hz)², λ_r = 1e−3 — which is also what keeps the simulation biologically plausible.

Rate→spike annealing. Train the Tier-C rate surrogate first, then anneal to spikes over 20k steps (quantization-clip-floor, Bu et al. 2022). This converts one very hard optimization problem into two easy ones. Assumption: the rate solution transfers with < 15% loss in top-1 agreement; measured at M2, and if it fails the anneal schedule is the first thing to lengthen.

6.3 Statistics

  • 2,000 games per opponent → ±25 Elo at 95% CI at typical draw rates. Full gauntlet = 8 opponents × 2,000 = 16,000 games.
  • SPRT, bounds [0, 10] Elo, α = β = 0.05, for checkpoint A/B during development — the Fishtest methodology. Typical stop 15k–40k games.
  • Openings: 8-ply balanced book (UHO_Lichess_4852_v1.epd), every opening played from both colours as a reverse pair so opening bias cancels exactly.
  • Time control: ChessFly's cost is fixed — one 96-step pass, ~0.5 ms on the 4090 — so the primary control is fixed nodes for the opponent (1k / 10k / 100k), with a secondary 60+0.6 wall-clock blitz set for realism. Report both.

6.4 Memorization vs. generalization

Four independent probes. Memorization is the most likely way this project produces a number that is real and meaningless.

  1. Temporal holdout. Split by game and by date; all eval games played after 2026-01-01. A train/test top-1 gap > 8 points is a flag.
  2. Position-novelty stratification. Zobrist-hash every eval position against the training set; report Elo and top-1 separately for seen vs. unseen, and plies <12 vs. ≥12. A >150-Elo gap means the opening book is doing the work.
  3. Chess960 transfer. A generalizer loses 150–250 Elo on shuffled back ranks; a memorizer collapses. A drop > 400 Elo means the model memorized opening theory rather than learning chess.
  4. Bilateral mirror test. Evaluate left–right mirrored positions (castling disabled to keep them legal). The connectome is bilaterally symmetric and both optic lobes receive the same board, so a model that learned board structure should be near-invariant. >100-Elo asymmetry means lateralized lookup, not spatial reasoning. This probe exists only because the substrate is a fly brain — it is free, and it is the sharpest test in the set.

07 Failure modes and kill criteria

Each is a measured number with a date. Hitting one means stop, not try harder.

KC-1 · CAPACITY WALL · M1, WEEK 6Stage-1 rate surrogate (Tier C — no spikes, the easiest possible version) plateaus at held-out top-1 < 30% with training loss still > 1.8 nats after 3 epochs. Tier C is a plain sparse RNN, so failure here indicts the topology, not the spiking. → The connectome cannot represent a chess policy at all. Kill, or relax the topology constraint and report that as the finding.
KC-2 · GRADIENT PATHOLOGY · M2, WEEK 10Either ‖∂L/∂h₀‖ / ‖∂L/∂h_T‖ outside [10⁻³, 10³], or per-parameter gradient SNR < 0.02 for more than 60% of weights. → BPTT is not reaching the recurrent core; the deep neuropils are being trained by noise. Kill BPTT, spend one 100-GPU-hour e-prop attempt, then kill.
KC-3 · INFORMATION BOTTLENECK · M2, WEEK 10Linear probe reconstructing piece placement from the narrowest population on the visual→central pathway scores < 85% (≈ <40 of ~160 board bits recovered) anywhere between medulla and central complex. → The board physically cannot reach the decision-making regions. Kill. This is the likeliest single point of failure: the fly's visual pathway is optimized for wide-field motion, not high-resolution static pattern.
KC-4 · ELO STALL · M6, WEEK 38< 1,200 Elo (CCRL-anchored, 2,000 games, ±25) with all stages complete. → The §2.4 ceiling was optimistic by more than its own stated floor. Ship as a demo, retract the Elo claim, publish the negative capacity result.
KC-5 · MOTOR DECODE DEGENERACY · M5, WEEK 30Unmasked legal-move rate < 40%. → 1,300 DNs cannot carry a factored move distribution; the masking relaxation is doing all the work. Relax to an external legality head and say so in every subsequent claim.
KC-6 · CONNECTOME-IRRELEVANCE CONTROL · M4, WEEK 22The adversarial control: train the identical pipeline on a degree-preserving rewired connectome (configuration model, same in/out degree sequence, shuffled targets). If the rewired network comes within 50 Elo of the real one, the biology contributed nothing and this is a paper about sparse RNNs. Not a kill — but it is the result, and it gets reported first, not buried.
